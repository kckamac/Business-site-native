Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about working with us.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects are completed within 3–4 weeks from the point of receiving your content and sign-off on the design direction. Larger enterprise projects may take 6–8 weeks. We provide a clear project timeline at the start of every engagement.

Do I need to provide the written content?

We recommend that clients provide their own copy, as you know your business best. However, we offer copywriting as an add-on service and can work with your existing brochures, social content, or talking points to produce polished, SEO-ready text.

Will my website work on mobile phones and tablets?

Absolutely. Every website we build is fully responsive by default — it adapts its layout for any screen size, from a small smartphone to a large desktop monitor. We test across iOS Safari, Android Chrome, and all major desktop browsers before delivery.

Can I update the website myself after it's built?

Yes. For clients who prefer self-management, we can integrate a headless CMS so you can update text, images, and blog posts without touching code. We also offer an affordable monthly maintenance plan if you'd prefer to leave updates to us.

What do you need from me to get started?

To begin, we need a completed brief (we provide a simple questionnaire), your brand assets (logo files, colours, preferred fonts if you have them), and a 50% deposit. From there, we handle everything — sitemap, wireframes, design, build, and launch.
